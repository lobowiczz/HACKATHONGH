Sessions

MotorLens is a screening and tracking aid, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose any condition. Compare results with your own earlier sessions rather than with other people. Video stays on this computer; only hand landmarks are analysed, and results are saved to a local file.
Amplitudes are measured in palm sizes, so results do not depend on camera distance. For best results use steady light, keep the whole hand in view and face the palm toward the camera.
