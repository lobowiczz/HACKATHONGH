Understanding the Invisible

Parkinson's Disease is a progressive nervous system disorder. By the time tremors appear, significant neural damage has occurred. Our AI looks deeper.

The Dopamine Deficit

Parkinson's is caused by the death of dopamine-producing neurons in the Substantia Nigra. This chemical messenger is vital for controlled movement. Our model detects subtle signal irregularities caused by this deficit.

Signal Biomarkers

We analyze complex features like Pitch Period Entropy (PPE), recurrence period density (R PDE), and specific frequency formants that change subtly in the early stages of neurodegeneration.

AI Precision

Using a custom Neural Network with dropout regularization, trained on clinical datasets, we can classify subjects with high accuracy based on inputs that are invisible to the naked eye.
