11 TOP WEBSITES

FOR IGCSE REVISION RESOURCES

There are a vast number of online resources available for students now thanks to the internet. We are here to share with our students 11 useful websites for IGCSE notes and resources for revision and examination preparation.

#02 YOUTUBE RESOURCE

The IGCSE Channel

The IGCSE Channel goes through IGCSE past year papers and helps students to get the model A* answers examiners are looking for. The host gives detailed solutions corresponding to the mark scheme. Additionally, she focuses on the three sciences of IGCSE - Biology, Chemistry, and Physics.

#03 YOUTUBE RESOURCE

CrashCourse

Formed by two brothers who wanted to make education easily accessible to everyone. The Crash Course team has produced more than 32 courses on a wide variety of subjects, including organic chemistry, literature, world history, biology, philosophy, theater, ecology, and many more! Their videos are concept-based and excellent for introductions or revision.

#04 YOUTUBE RESOURCE

Michel van Biezen

Michel van Biezen is a channel hosted by a professor containing a variety of subject videos which include Physics, Chemistry, Astronomy, Math, and Mechanical Engineering. His videos explain fundamental concepts as well as examples to solve questions and problems step-by-step.

#05 REVISION NOTES

IGCSE Pro

IGCSE Pro is a website that offers students resources specifically for IGCSE Biology and ICT. They have complete and comprehensive notes for all the sub-topics under Biology and ICT. A great place to check if you need clarification on specific chapters.

#06 SCIENCE RESOURCE

Stamp Science

Stamp Science offers notes on the three IGCSE sciences for years 7, 8, and 9 students. Their website breaks down the subjects into sub-topics and includes essential formulas, concepts, and key takeaways. They also use simulations for several topics to better illustrate difficult concepts to students.

#07 SCIENCE RESOURCE

Science with Hazel

Science with Hazel is hosted by Hazel, who explains GCSE and IGCSE science topics including the new Grade 1 to 9 syllabus. She answers practice exam papers in all three sciences - Chemistry, Biology, and Physics - to help students walk through their exam revision effectively.

#08 REVISION NOTES

Notes4IGCSE

Notes4IGCSE mainly provides students with IGCSE Biology and Chemistry Notes. Their notes are detailed and easy to read, covering all sub-topics of the subject in detail. It is great for students who are looking for quick summary notes to refresh their memory.

#09 BLOG & NOTES

TeachifyMe

TeachifyMe offers subject notes for the majority of IGCSE subjects. They also provide blog pages that go through example questions. This page is extremely helpful for finding practice materials. Note: To gain access to their full premium notes, payment may be required.

#10 HUMANITIES

OxNotes

OxNotes contains study and revision notes for many GCSE subjects including English Literature, Geography, and History. Their study guides help students go through each concept and chapter, often including recommended YouTube videos. It serves as a complete study guide for students in the humanities.

#11 OFFICIAL SOURCE

Cambridge Assessment

OFFICIAL SITE >

The official IGCSE website is undeniably one of the most useful websites for revision. It provides students with past-year examination papers that are available for download. Past year papers are extremely helpful for revision, and the mark schemes provided serve as the ultimate guide to your preparation.
