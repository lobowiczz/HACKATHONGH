# Hackathon idea bank: Climate Tech, Massachusetts

Reference this file with `#hackathon-ideas` in Kiro chat. Data sources were suggested from memory and **not yet verified live**; check availability and terms before building on any of them.

## Track 3 brief
Resilient communities in a changing climate; cleaner, more affordable buildings; a more reliable clean energy system; understanding and protecting the ocean; sustainable blue economy and coastal livelihoods.

## Ideas

| Theme | Idea | Massachusetts angle | Candidate data | Hand UI fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resilient communities | Coastal flood and heat explorer: set sea-level rise and surge, see exposed homes, roads, heat-vulnerable neighborhoods | Revere, Chelsea, Scituate, New Bedford | Resilient MA clearinghouse, NOAA sea-level viewer, MassGIS environmental-justice layers | Weak: drag a water level on a wall display |
| Buildings | Triple-decker retrofit planner: weatherization and heat pumps by cost, savings, emissions; landlord vs renter split | Mass Save rebates, Boston BERDO, Cambridge BEUDO | BERDO/BEUDO public datasets | Weak |
| Clean energy | Clean-hours advisor: when grid power is cleanest and cheapest for EVs, heat pumps, laundry | ISO-NE grid | ISO-NE web services (free account) | Weak |
| Ocean | Estuary and acidification watch | Gulf of Maine warming, Cape Cod and Buzzards Bay nitrogen and acidification | NERACOOS and NOAA buoys, Buzzards Bay Coalition data | Optional |
| Blue economy | **Shellfish Watch**: grower early-warning plus hands-free field log | Wellfleet, Duxbury, Barnstable; New Bedford fishing port | Mass DMF closures, buoys, bloom (red tide) advisories | **Strong**: wet, gloved hands |

## Recommendation
Shellfish Watch. It covers two themes, reuses the existing Ocean Acidification site and the gesture engine, has a concrete user, and is the one idea where the camera does real work. Runner-up: the flood explorer for visual demo impact.
